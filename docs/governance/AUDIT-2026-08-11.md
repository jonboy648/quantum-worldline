## 1. Executive verdict

Quantum Worldline is, as of today, a well-instrumented **numerical CFT validation program with two
usable methodological products and one submitted manuscript whose central claim does not survive
re-analysis**. Its pipelines work: three independent auditors re-derived the raw numbers and they
held, and one re-implemented the Yang-Lee driver in a second language and reproduced it to six
decimals (`inv-pipelines.md` §5.2). But of 34 claims involving a calculation, **21 could not have
come out otherwise** — conformal symmetry, a Virasoro identity, BPZ 1984, or the definition
`l_AdS = 1/ln χ` fixed the answer before the run (`claim-evidence-map.md` §1.2). **No Level-4
content exists anywhere in the program, and no Level-3 claim survives its gate.**

The strongest defensible contribution is **methodological**: the Z₂/Z₃-symmetric optimizer that
converted a basin lottery into reliable convergence, the two-phase ALM+projection VQE, the
exact-diagonalization pre-gates, and the documented **inversion of the unitary state-identification
rule at Yang-Lee** — the one thing nothing predicted (`derivation-gate.md` §9.6 R3).

The biggest scientific risk is **framing outrunning evidence in an artifact already under review**:
the PRD manuscript's α = 3.92 ± 0.16 is a fitting artifact (95.8 % of the fit weight on one point,
`skeptical-referee.md` §3.1), and its only claim of external validation is absent from the
project's own record of the cited paper.

## 7. The geometry question

§4 of the standard names this the most important research question in the project. Two adversaries
were assigned to it with opposite briefs. Both returned findings that damage the project's current
geometric claims — including the advocate, which is the fact that decides this section.

### 7.1 The skeptic's case (Hypothesis A: geometry is descriptive)

`geometry-skeptic.md`, quantity by quantity under §34:

1. **The transport measurement never applies a MERA.** `holographic_transport_16q.py:347`:
   `"Skipping full MERA bulk computation (complex to implement correctly)"`. The tensors are built,
   unitarity-checked, and unused. The 24 q and fine-dt scripts never build them. The measured object
   is a **lattice light-cone crossing time on a boundary spin chain**.
2. **The one constant carrying all the geometry is declared by the manuscript itself to be a
   representation property** — "set by the MERA architecture, not by optimization" — and the same
   paper's own χ=3 result gives `l_AdS = 0.9102`, moving the transport prediction to 1.430 against a
   measurement of 2.239: **+57 %**. A quantity that changes 37 % when you *improve* the
   representation of the identical state is, by §34, a property of the representation.
3. **α = 4 is proved analytically inside this repository from BPZ 1984 fusion rules**, with no
   geometry in the derivation (`research/data/theory/epsilon-channel-proof.md`), and measured on an
   exactly diagonalized chain with no network. The χ=3 MERA agrees because it is a good
   approximation to that state — which measures **ansatz fidelity**, not bulk physics.
4. **Four independent attempts to make a bulk-geometric object do dynamical work all failed**, and
   the one recorded as "SUPPORTED" succeeded by proving it cannot: HYP-012 NOT_SUPPORTED (predicted
   profile *rises* where the measured one falls), HYP-014 REFUTED (5863 % max residual; the 1.6 %
   amplitude "agreement" a coincidence), HYP-022 REFUTED (α = 0.883; the "correct"
   Kirillov-Kostant measure changed the exponent by **1.2 %** — a numerical no-op), HYP-026
   SUPPORTED-but-force-blind (κ = 0.243 ≈ 2Δ_σ, not 4). Under Hypothesis A this outcome is forced:
   geometric re-parameterizations of boundary data are measure-preserving relabellings and cannot
   generate an exponent the underlying operator does not have.
5. **The §34 table has no entry that is both representation-sensitive and empirically determined.**
   Everything measured is a state property recoverable from the boundary; everything network-specific
   (`l_AdS`, `d_geo`) is asserted from the architecture and multiplied in. And `d_geo` has **two
   mutually incompatible definitions used in the same manuscript** — linear (`dr·l_AdS`, for the
   worldline/m_eff argument) and logarithmic (`2·l_AdS·ln dr`, for the power-law argument) — which
   predict exponential and power-law forces respectively.

### 7.2 The advocate's case (Hypothesis B: geometry is operational)

`geometry-advocate.md` was briefed to make the strongest possible case. It made three moves, and the
second is the most consequential finding in this section.

1. **It sharpened B into something falsifiable.** Under a literal reading of §4 — where A already
   grants "the CFT **and tensor-network structure**" — an observable that is a functional of the
   state alone is A's, and an observable that is a functional of the network alone is *also* A's.
   B has content in exactly one band: **joint functionals of (state, network)**. The operational form
   is **B★**: invariance under gauge / architecture / χ, *plus* non-derivability from {χ, b, cone
   width} or {c, Δ, C_ijk}, *plus* a parameter-free out-of-sample prediction. **All three clauses are
   required, and no observable currently in the project satisfies even the first two.**
2. **It produced new evidence against the project's own strongest geometric anchor.** The advocate
   diagonalized the Wang-He boundary Hamiltonian itself (code included, reproducible in < 30 s) and
   found the gaps **are** the Ising scaling dimensions unrescaled: 0.12510, 0.99839, 1.11869,
   1.97760 at N = 16 versus 1/8, 1, 9/8, 2. E₀ matches the transport records to 6 decimals,
   confirming the same operator. **In that normalization the boundary lives on a circle of
   circumference 2π with v = 1, boundary time is global CFT cylinder time, and `l_AdS` cancels from
   the null-geodesic crossing time.** The ℓ-free prediction is π/2 = 1.571 or π = 3.142 against a
   measurement of 2.239. **The reported 1.2 % agreement is supplied entirely by the free factor
   1/ln 2.** The advocate tested four rescue arguments and none survives.
3. **It found the manuscript carries three incompatible AdS radii** for the same claimed geometry —
   1.4427 (χ=2, transport), 0.9102 (χ=3, force), 0.4024 (χ=12, Potts) — because Eq. (2) makes
   `l_AdS` a function of the bond dimension. Had the transport comparison used the project's *best*
   MERA, the prediction would be 0.632 against 2.239.
4. **Of §11's eight candidate geometry tests, five cannot discriminate A from B even in principle**
   (A: locality is exact by isometry — a theorem about the ansatz, granted to A; C: modular flow is
   CHM-fixed; D: an MPS is *exactly* Markov, so the control does not separate; E: the solved
   deformations have closed-form CFT answers; G: three independent in-project refutations already).
   Three survive: **H (rewiring, rank 1), B (entanglement-wedge, rank 2), F (c-scaling of transport,
   rank 3)**.

### 7.3 The editor's ruling

**Is the distinction currently well-posed inside this project? No.** Not because the question is
bad — §7.2 shows it can be made sharp — but because the project has (i) **no fixed definition of
bulk distance** (two incompatible ones in one manuscript), (ii) **no equivalence relation** saying
when two networks have the same geometry, (iii) **two conflicting definitions of `l_AdS`** (1/ln χ
vs 1/ln K, coinciding only for the binary χ=2 network the project has largely stopped using), and
(iv) **no observable that depends on the network's internal organization.** §33's closing line
applies exactly: *the program needs a sharper definition before it needs a larger simulation.*

**Is it operationally testable? Yes, cheaply, and the design exists.** The skeptic and the advocate
converged independently on the same experiment from opposite directions — the skeptic's
"matched-ansatz rewiring test" and the advocate's **GEO-1** are the same experiment with the same
killers. Both name the transport arm as the cheapest first slice, and both pre-register their
failure criteria. §40 asks for the smallest experiment that most changes the project's
decision-making; this is it.

**Ruling on the current claims.** Hypothesis A is **unrefuted and is the parsimonious reading of the
project's own records**. That is not "A is proven" — the skeptic says so explicitly and is right to.
But three facts are now established and are not in dispute between the two adversaries:

- **No experiment capable of separating A from B has ever been run in this repository.** No
  rewiring (`grep -ril "rewir" research/` → nothing), no gauge-invariance test, no matched
  non-geometric control.
- **Every geometric quantity in the manuscript reduces along the dependency graph to `l_AdS = 1/ln χ`
  (a definition) or Δ_T = 2 (a theorem).** That is §12's *independent-prediction killer*, and on
  present evidence **it has fired.**
- **The advocate — not the skeptic — supplied the argument that the flagship geometric number's
  `l_AdS` factor is dimensionally spurious.** When the side assigned to defend a claim produces the
  strongest evidence against it, the claim should not be carried forward in its current form.

**Consequences.** (a) `README.md:5`'s "quantitative gravitational content" and the manuscript's
opening "MERA … encodes an emergent AdS geometry" must be demoted from Result to Motivation until
GEO-1 reports. (b) The advocate's unit argument is an **analytic inference, not a completed proof**
(the advocate says so), and it must be independently re-derived before it is asserted in print — but
it bears directly on Result 2 of a manuscript **currently with editors**, and that timing is an
editorial judgement for the director, not a technical one for this audit. (c) The geometry program
should not be funded further until GEO-1 slices 0–1 report; they cost hours and no cluster.

## 10. Adjudications — where the artifacts disagree

### 10.1 Is `research/data/theory/` empty?

**§14's diagnosis stands; its statement of fact does not.**

### 10.2 Is "solve `Hᵀw = Ew` directly" the most informative missing Yang-Lee test?

**Disagreement.** `inv-pipelines.md` §10 item 2 ranks it "the single most informative missing test
of the biorthogonal layer, ~1 hour". `alternative-explanations.md` §3.2(iv) proves it is a **no-op**:
`build_Hn` produces a **complex-symmetric** matrix, so `Hᵀ = H` and the true left row-eigenvector
*is* `vᵀ`; and with PT unbroken, `P conj(v) = e^{iθ}v`, so the code's `v†P` differs from `vᵀ` by a
per-state phase that cancels in the ratio.

**Ruling: `alternative-explanations.md` is right.** It gives an explicit algebraic argument from the
builder's own structure, and the same artifact proves the stronger statement that `c_KS` is
invariant under the **entire** pseudo-Hermitian metric family `η → η·p(H)`, including the
positive-definite CPT metric. The recommendation is therefore replaced in §9 by the **weakly
non-Hermitian Ising control** (λ = 4, small h, known c = 1/2), which turns a binary Hermitian /
non-Hermitian switch into a continuous dial with a known answer at every setting.

### 10.3 Is the Yang-Lee sign a high-novelty measurement, or forced?

**Disagreement.** `claim-evidence-map.md`, `independence-ledger.md` and `alternative-explanations.md`
call the sign the program's **only high-counterfactual-novelty measured bit**.
`derivation-gate.md` §9.4 derives in four lines that, given (A1) KS convergence, (A2) the bilinear
pairing, (A3) the state identification and (A4) Δ_T = 2, `c_KS = c` **identically**, that the sign is
forced with it, and that `c_eff = c − 24h_min` **has no expression inside the vacuum Virasoro
module** — so "c_eff EXCLUDED" is a *vacuous* exclusion of a hypothesis the estimator could not have
confirmed under any state assignment.

**Ruling: both, and the record conflates them.** The derivation is correct and I adopt it. The
resolution is that **the counterfactual novelty lives in whether (A1)–(A2) hold at all off
unitarity, not in the number**. Koo-Saleur is a lattice→continuum *conjecture* even in the unitary
case; nothing forces it to survive a non-normal operator with an indefinite pairing. So:

- "we measured c = −22/5 with its sign" → **near-zero novelty, Level 1.**
- "the KS correspondence survives non-unitarity, evidenced by a monotone negative ladder" →
  **moderate-to-high novelty, Level 2 aspiring to 3 — and this is the claim to make.**
- "c_eff = +2/5 EXCLUDED" → **not a physics discrimination.** It is a useful check that the estimator
  did not return garbage, and should be reported as that.

Note the derivation gate also sharpens the *worry*: because the sign is
`sign(⟨T_L|T_R⟩⟨0_L|0_R⟩)` and the numerator is a perfect square in the exact limit, the negative
sign is **conditional on the pairing choice (A2)**. Under the Hermitian inner product the same data
give a positive `c_KS`. The pairing is the right algebraic object — but the record's line
*"sign is now part of the measurement, not a convention"* is true only relative to an input.

### 10.4 The puMPS↔ED cross-check: 7 % disagreement or 5e−6 agreement?

**Ruling: 5e−6 agreement, once the convention is matched.** `VENDOR.md:50` compares puMPS's raw
N=12 value against SIM-067's *extrapolated* number, concealing a 7.2 % same-N gap in `c`.
`inv-pipelines.md` §3.4 diagnosed the cause (naive whole-term KS phase vs the half-bond convention
used by upstream ZMV, the Potts ED gate and the Yang-Lee driver) and **measured the fix**: rebuilding
SIM-067's ED with the half-bond phase gives c = 0.497151 at N=12 against puMPS's 0.497156.
`independence-ledger.md` L-10 adds that the Δ_T row of the same table is already a matched-N
agreement at 7e−5 and is not identified as one. **The project owns its strongest internal
independence result and records it as a discrepancy.**

### 10.5 Did the C_TTT ladder measure a three-point function?

**Ruling: no, and no record says the object changed.** `inv-sims-late.md` F1,
`inv-pipelines.md` §3.3/§4.1, `derivation-gate.md` §4.2, `novelty-methods.md` §2.2 and
`dependency-graph.md` CIRC-5 all independently locate the literal line
`C_TTT = 2*sqrt(2)/sqrt(c)` in every driver. Route A — the genuine spin-resolved ⟨TTT⟩ contraction —
was attempted and **decisively failed** (`SIM-2026-066.json`: gates satisfiable exactly while the
number is arbitrary), and the equal-time correlator route was falsified by ED the day it was
designed. **Route B silently substituted a two-point observable for the three-point one, and the
substitution is recorded nowhere.** The design doc's own honest sentence — that this measurement is
"of the same epistemic kind as C_σσε = 0.5" — exists only in the design doc, not in the headlines.

### 10.6 A physics disagreement the project has not noticed

`derivation-gate.md` §3.4 derives, from the Kac formula, that at the **tricritical Ising** point the
fusion ε × ε = 1 + ε′ places a scalar at Δ_ε′ = 6/5 = 1.2 **below** the stress tensor, so the
conformal-block mechanism the manuscript itself advances predicts **α = 2.4 at TCI, not 4** (at
Potts the leading non-identity scalar in the ε channel is at Δ = 2.8 > 2, so α = 4 *is* the correct
prediction there, for a ℤ₃-neutral exactly-ε probe).

**Ruling: this is a genuine, previously unrecorded prediction, and it inverts the universality
argument.** The quantity the project validated (Δ_T = 2) could not have varied; the quantity that
*could* have varied — the exchange channel, and hence the observable exponent — predicts a
**class-dependent** α and was never measured at two of three classes. It also supplies the named
killer §9 item 12 needs: **α ≈ 2.4 at TCI would confirm the mechanism and refute the universality
slogan; α ≈ 4 would be a genuine surprise requiring explanation.** Either outcome changes a
decision. *(Flagged UNVERIFIED at §11.6: this is one agent's derivation, checked against the Kac
formula but not against the literature or a second auditor.)*

## 12. Closing

Three sentences the director can act on.

**The program's instruments are sound and its record-keeping is not.** Every number an auditor could
reach was reproduced; almost no number can be reached from the public artifact the manuscript points
at, and the structured records can no longer answer which hypotheses are refuted.

**The science that is guaranteed has been oversold and the science that is not has been
under-defended.** Twenty-one of 34 calculated claims could not have come out otherwise; the four

caught its own script's false positive and named the field `verdict_reason_AUTO_WAS_WRONG`; SIM-034

disciplined program would have published; SIM-064 discarded its *tighter* number for the honest one;
SIM-053 overrode its own automated "REFUTED (stronger universality)" verdict downward on a structural
argument. That capability is worth more than the paper it is currently attached to, and it is the
reason the corrections in §9 are achievable rather than aspirational.
