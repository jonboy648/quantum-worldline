## Recommended rewordings

- **R1 (§2 Claim; row 8).** Replace "So the relevant contrast is not duality versus emergence but state property
  versus representation property" with: "That literature already reads exact duals as different representations of one
  theory (Dieks et al. 2015, §3.3.2) and puts 'representation and redundancy in representation' at the core of
  AdS/CFT (Rickles 2013). Tensor networks sharpen the point: the network is one of many non-unique, approximate
  representations of a single state, not an exact dual theory, so the live contrast is state property versus
  representation property." Also cite Rickles beside Redhead in §3 and in the Limitations novelty hedge.
- **R2 (§8; row 2).** Replace "which is emergence in Butterfield's weak sense at most" with "which would make it at
  most novel and robust behaviour relative to the class of networks on that graph (Butterfield's definition), not a
  property of the state". If the finite-chi point is meant, say it in his terms: sharp cut-like recovery is a
  large-bond-dimension limit, and at chi = 6 one has at most the finite-N analogue, which Butterfield counts as the
  physically real sense.
- **R3 (§5; row 14).** Replace "This is Mayo's severity applied before the data; unlike use-novelty, the issue is
  whether the hypothesis could have failed, not whether the data built it" with: "This is Mayo's minimal severity
  requirement, checked before the data. Agreement from a method 'practically guaranteed to find such agreement' is
  'bad evidence, no test' (Mayo 2018, §1.1). Against use-novelty, what matters is 'not whether passing is assured but
  whether erroneous passing is' (Mayo 1996, pp. 274-275): would the run have disagreed had the interpretation been
  false?"
- **R4 (§6 Caution; row 17).** Replace "Stegenga's discordance point: independence is necessary for robustness to
  confirm, and not sufficient" with: "Stegenga (2009): robustness 'requires ... knowing that multiple modes of evidence
  are independent ... and yet remains fallible' (p. 654), since concordant independent evidence can still support an
  incorrect conclusion. His separate discordance problem (§3) is that real evidence is usually discordant and
  robustness gives no rule for weighing it."
- **R5 (§8; row 18).** Replace "a lattice model can confirm claims about a target only under an independently argued
  universality assumption" with: "an analogue system can confirm claims about an inaccessible target only through
  'model-external and empirically grounded arguments', in their case universality arguments plus diverse realisations
  of the effect (Dardashti, Thébault and Winsberg 2017, §1)".
- **R6 (§2 Move; rows 21, 23, 24).** Replace "Then the physics of MERA-as-geometry (Swingle; Vidal) and its critics
  (Bao et al.; Milsted and Vidal's light-cone reading; Beny on causal structure)" with: "Then the MERA ansatz (Vidal
  2007); its reading as discrete AdS (Swingle 2012); alternative geometric readings (Beny 2013, de Sitter causal
  structure; Milsted and Vidal 2018, light sheet or light cone rather than the hyperbolic plane); and constraints on an
  AdS/MERA correspondence (Bao et al. 2015)."

  "killed: pre-empted by prior art on MERA geometry (arXiv:1812.00529, arXiv:1504.06632) and refuted as stated by the
