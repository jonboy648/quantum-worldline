Full agent-team sweep on Matthew Kinsella, Infleqtion, Rydberg sensing, and the "quantum tricorder" claim. Three findings worth keeping.

3. Through-rock person detection is plausible at 1–30 m via OPM, not at mountain scale.

ULF skin depth in granite at 1 Hz is ~16 km, so ULF biomagnetic signals penetrate rock essentially unattenuated. The bottleneck is sensor noise and dipole r⁻³ falloff: heart MCG drops from 50 pT at body surface to ~50 fT at 10 m, ~50 aT at 100 m. Current best OPM (QuSpin QZFM Gen-3, 10 fT/√Hz) hits SNR ~39 at 10 m with 10-min integration. Mountain-scale (km) is physically impossible.
