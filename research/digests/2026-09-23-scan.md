## Findings by class

### Catalogued (in-window)

- **PAPER-2026-146 — arXiv:2608.20156 (Liu, 08-20), rel 4, HYP-028.** *Biorthogonal Conformal Dynamics
  in Non-Hermitian Quantum Quenches.* BCFT for global quenches in the **interacting Yang-Lee spin chain**,
  left covector treated as part of the protocol, statically calibrated boundary data predicting real-time
  biorthogonal dynamics at 1e-3; also a complex five-state Potts point. Same model and pairing as
  SIM-070/071, and it calibrates boundary data the way SIM-070 calibrated g1/R_∞. It measures no central
  charge, so it adds nothing to the retracted c_KS claim, but it is now **mandatory cite-adjacent prior art
  for any biorthogonal Yang-Lee boundary or dynamics result** we produce. Abstract only.
- **PAPER-2026-147 — arXiv:2608.27564 (Bertuzzo, Corradini, Frugiuele, 08-27), rel 3.** Finite-BCH
  polynomial entanglers in **wavelet (continuum) MERA** are stuck at mean-field (β = 1/2, never 1/8).
  A continuum no-go only; our lattice MERAs reach Ising/TCI/Potts data, so no anchor is touched. Matters
  only if the non-unitary cMERA fallback for HYP-028 is revived.
- **PAPER-2026-148 — arXiv:2609.07866 (Alipour Shahmiri, Sharifian, Vardian, 09-07), rel 3, HYP-033-adjacent.**
  Entanglement wedge reconstruction beyond large N via the **twirled Petz map**, with an explicit
  subleading bi-local Petz-kernel correction. Continuum AdS/CFT; silent on where recovery fails on a
  finite network. No effect on HYP-033.
- **PAPER-2026-149 — arXiv:2608.28962 (Elimelech, Albert, Barg, 08-29), rel 3, HYP-033-adjacent.**
  Universal recovery in AQEC: one recovery map (the error-set Petz map) corrects every channel controlled
  by an error set, with guarantees in environment-leakage and Knill-Laflamme Hellinger distances. Could
  convert HYP-033's decoupling number into a recovery-error bound after unblinding; does not bear on the
  cone-vs-cut statistic. Companion 2607.22995 not checked.
- **PAPER-2026-150 — arXiv:2608.19428 (Yi-Yu Lin, Song Cheng, 08-19), rel 3.** Bit threads as Coecke
  quantum-information-flow trajectories on holographic tensor networks; ZX analysis of stabilizer
  networks sees structure beyond entanglement entropy. Same first author as PAPER-036. A network-level
  observable (the class §29 says we lack), but for stabilizer networks, not an optimized CFT MERA. WATCH
  for a MERA version.
- **PAPER-2026-151 — arXiv:2608.26082 (Yan, 08-26), rel 3, HYP-028.** Yang-Lee criticality realized
  without post-selection as a dissipative dynamical transition from local unitaries + 2-qubit engineered
  dissipation. A possible experimental leg for Yang-Lee physics; supplies no lattice CFT data.
