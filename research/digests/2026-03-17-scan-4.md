### PAPER-2026-036 — Lin, "Thread embodiment of holographic quantum entanglement" (NOVEL, relevance 4)
- **arXiv:** 2501.10691, published in *Physical Review* (APS), 2026
- **Status:** NOVEL — new framework for the geometry of holographic entanglement in MERA
- **Key insight:** Holographic quantum entanglement can be visualized as thread-like objects flowing through the MERA tensor network, forming a "preset ensemble of wires" with flow-conserving properties.
- **Connection to our project:**
  - The threads are the causal channels through which our holographic transport signal propagates (HYP-2026-006)
  - Thread discreteness at finite L=16 could account for the 0.72% transport time residual we measured
  - The flow-conservation property is exactly the MERA unitary structure (Evenbly & White 2016) that makes our circuits work
  - **This paper could provide a new theoretical angle on why the transport time T=(pi/2)*l_AdS is so precisely predicted: threads enforce the causal light-cone crossing condition geometrically**

- **Status:** WATCH — relevant background, not immediately actionable
- **Key insight:** Penalty factors on conformal group generators create a richer complexity geometry; MERA is explicitly discussed as a realization. Different generator weights = different operator types (scalar vs stress tensor).
