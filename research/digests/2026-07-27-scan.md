## Findings by class

### NOVEL / high relevance — CATALOGUED
- **PAPER-2026-138** (2607.21536, 2026-07-23, rel 4, quant-ph + cond-mat.stat-mech + hep-th).
  Near an exceptional point the **biorthogonal** entanglement entropy of a finite non-Hermitian critical
  chain carries an **interval-independent residual** `S_res = log(Δ·L)` on top of Calabrese-Cardy, visible
  even for a **one-site** subsystem, and retaining sensitivity to a gap Δ < 1/L. Explicitly **no Hermitian
  analogue**. Explained geometrically: the non-unitary cMERA dS bulk has an extremal (RT) surface that
  reaches the **IR endpoint**, so entanglement keeps the endpoint contribution; on a finite ring a regular
  circuit cannot disentangle to a one-site product state and leaves an entangled two-site IR state whose
  entanglement reproduces the same `log(Δ·L)`.

  **Two usable items for HYP-028** (written back into the hypothesis record this session):
  1. **A cheap new diagnostic.** `S_res = log(Δ·L)` is interval-independent and single-site-detectable —
     a candidate low-cost regime indicator for the existing biorthogonal pipeline
     (`research/data/simulations/hyp028_nonunitary_force.py`). It is a **supplement to, not a substitute
     for, the mandatory G4 gate** (c_eff = 2/5 *and* Δ_φ = −1/5): an offset shows non-Hermitian criticality
     is being felt, not that the Yang-Lee fixed point has been reached.
  2. **A structural note on IR termination** — finite-depth circuits on a ring leave a two-site entangled
     IR state. That is a remark about the top of the causal cone our force / OPE / C_TTT pipelines contract
     through, so it is worth a glance at our own top-of-cone treatment.

  **HONEST LIMIT — this does NOT unblock HYP-028.** The construction lives **near exceptional points**,
  which is the same PT-edge neighborhood that produced the SIM-053/055 obstruction (at ED sizes the
  Yang-Lee fixed point only appears *after* PT-breaking). It supplies no non-unitary fixed point sitting at
  finite distance **inside** the PT-unbroken window with documented c_eff/Δ_φ. The preferred next concrete
  step is **unchanged** from the scan-#14 note: reproduce PAPER-2026-133's (2606.16785) periodic PT-chain /
  left-dual Yang-Lee boundary-data extraction on our lattice plumbing *before* building any non-Hermitian
  MERA — now with `S_res = log(Δ·L)` added as a secondary diagnostic.
