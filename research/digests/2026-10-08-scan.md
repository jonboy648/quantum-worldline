## Findings by class

### Catalogued (in-window)

- **PAPER-2026-152 — arXiv:2610.08787 (Meng, Lan, Gu, 10-06), rel 4, ACTIONABLE.** *Algebraic Tensor
  Network Renormalization and Holographic duality.* Fusion-category constraints built into Loop-TNR
  tensors. Untuned initial tensors flow to stable gapless fixed points for Ising, TCI and Potts; conformal
  towers are resolved by anyon sector; sector-resolved structure-constant magnitudes are extracted. Full
  text grep-read. Facts that matter for us:
  - Ising at χ_RG = 36: |C_σσε| = **0.500424** (ours, SIM-061: 0.5075).
  - Accuracy depends on **three fitted "collar length" parameters per model** (Ising: fitted from 24
    table entries). Out-of-fit rms error is 0.060% Ising / 0.157% TCI / 0.399% Potts with the fit, and
    0.50% / 2.49% / 3.84% without it. SIM-061 fits nothing, so any comparison has to state that
    asymmetry.
  - **No stress-tensor three-point data**: T T̄ appears only as an irrelevant correction. Descendant
    accuracy is explicitly weaker. So it does **not** pre-empt the C_TTT ladder.
  - "Holographic" here means a **Turaev-Viro / topological bulk**, not an emergent AdS geometry.
  - Same group lineage as PAPER-2026-135 (c=1 fixed-point tensors, HYP-030).
- **PAPER-2026-153 — arXiv:2610.00478 (Sahu, van der Heijden, Van Raamsdonk, Zibakhsh, 09-30; v2 10-02),
  rel 4, WATCH, HYP-033.** *Algebraic structure in holographic tensor networks.* In isometric / large-D
  random tensor networks, regions with minimal boundary-leg count ("generalized entanglement wedge"
  analogues) carry canonical boundary subalgebras. Intersections, joins and commutants track geometric
  operations on regions; non-isometric maps "proceed through a minimal cut". A type II_1 algebra is built
  as an ultraproduct over growing D. Full text grep-read: **exact isometry is assumed, finite-D approximate
  isometries are explicitly set aside, and there is no MERA, no optimized network, and no numerics.**
- **PAPER-2026-154 — arXiv:2609.38815 (Mori, Yoshida, 09-30), rel 3, WATCH.** Holographic relative
  entropy of entanglement = tripartition-surface area − RT area. Upper bound proved for general random
  tensor networks, lower bound for 1-3 Haar tensors. An entropic (state-functional) quantity with a
  minimal-surface dual in random networks. Context for HYP-033's Haar control. Abstract only.
- **PAPER-2026-155 — arXiv:2609.33567 (Makwana et al., 09-27), rel 3, WATCH.** Inverse **binary** MERA
  state learning on an open-boundary TFIM ground state with terminal-register measurement. 8 qubits:
  mean fidelity 0.9969. 16 qubits: MERA beats a parameter-matched, causal-cone-complete local circuit in
  30/30 paired comparisons, but **no run met their stationarity criterion**. The matched-control design is
  a usable template for a hardware MERA-prep leg; the evidence itself is weak. Abstract only.

## Math-bridge connections (lead-run; interpretive, not results)

1. **Two unrelated meanings of "holographic tensor network" landed in the same week.** PAPER-152's bulk is
   topological (Turaev-Viro / SymTO). It reproduces sector-resolved CFT data to 0.1% with no geometry at
   all. PAPER-153's bulk is geometric (min-cut regions ↔ subalgebras), but that is established only in an
   idealized network. HYP-033 found that an optimized χ=6 CFT network shows no cut-structured recovery. Read
   together, this bears on the trunk question (RESEARCH-DIRECTOR §29): **accurate CFT data does not need a
   geometric bulk, and the geometric bulk structure shows up where the network is idealized, not where it
   is optimized to the CFT at accessible χ.** Those are three independent sources, but it is a pattern in
   the literature plus one Level-2 result, not evidence for any claim. A philosophy-of-physics piece on
   "state functionals vs network-sensitive observables" would have to cite PAPER-152 and 153 (plus
   PAPER-150, Lin-Cheng ZX bit threads, from scan #20).
2. **Independence-ledger note (§7) for any future comparison against PAPER-152:** their accuracy is
   partly calibrated (collar lengths fitted on the same table they report). Out-of-fit channels are the
   fair comparison set, and they report those separately. Use those numbers.
3. **Petz thread:** Petz recovery now appears in Gibbs-state preparation (2610.01190, 2609.39674) as a
   *constructive* local recovery primitive. That is not network-geometric, and it does not connect to
   HYP-033's decoupling statistic without a derivation. No action.
