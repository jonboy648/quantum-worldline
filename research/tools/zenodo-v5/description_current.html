CORRECTION NOTICE (v5, 2026-08-24). The central result of v1–v4 is WITHDRAWN.

What is wrong. The force exponent reported in earlier versions as alpha = 3.92 +/- 0.16 is an artifact of the fitting procedure, not a property of the data. The fit was unweighted least squares in linear space applied to a steeply falling profile, which places 95.8 percent of its objective on the single point at the shortest retained separation. Re-fitting the same data with nothing discarded, changing only the error model, gives 2.82 (relative-error weighting) or 2.89 (log-space regression). Across fifteen combinations of standard estimator and fit window the exponent spans 2.53 to 3.92, and the previously published value is the largest of them.

What the data actually show. The corrected measurement is alpha = 2.6 +/- 0.2, a genuine plateau in the local logarithmic slope over six consecutive separations, since reproduced independently at 2.5921 by a separately written implementation. This value matches neither stress-tensor exchange (2*Delta_T = 4) nor scalar exchange (2*Delta_epsilon = 2), so the exchange channel is an open question rather than an identification.

Also withdrawn. (1) The claim that the exponent is universal across the three CFT classes: the exponent was measured only at Ising, and the tricritical and Potts results are accurate measurements of the operator spectra, not of the force exponent. (2) The third “independent evaluation”, alpha = 4.07, whose profile changes sign within its own fit window and cannot be described by a fixed-sign power law. (3) The geometric reading of the transport result, disputed on two independent grounds and now unresolved.

Not previously reported. The published fit has a reduced chi-squared of 6.008 on 9 degrees of freedom, and a bootstrap standard deviation of 0.63 rather than the quoted 0.16. Both were computed before release and were omitted.

What is unaffected. The two-phase ALM + Z2-projection optimizer, the chi = 3 criticality threshold, the exact-diagonalization gates, the measured OPE coefficient C_sigma-sigma-epsilon = 0.50 +/- 0.03 against the exact 1/2, the conformal towers at all three classes, and the C_TTT stress-tensor self-coupling values (3.9936 / 3.381 / 3.163 against 4.000 / 3.3806 / 3.16228).

Provenance. The error was found by an internal adversarial audit after submission. This work was not peer-reviewed: it was submitted to Physical Review D and rejected on 2026-08-12, on separate grounds and before this error was found. The two facts are independent and should not be conflated. Files in this record include the full re-analysis (SIM-2026-072), the pre-registered follow-up channel test (SIM-2026-073), the error register (ERRATA.md) and runnable drivers.

The abstract of the original manuscript follows unchanged, for the record. Every claim in it about the force exponent should be read against the correction above.
