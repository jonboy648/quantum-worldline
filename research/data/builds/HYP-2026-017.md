## Aquila Platform Specs

- **Type**: Analog Rydberg tweezer array (NOT gate-based)
- **Atoms**: Up to 256 programmable 87Rb sites
- **Blockade radius**: ~9.76 um at Omega/2pi = 1 MHz (|70S1/2> state, C6 = 862 GHz*um^6)
- **Control**: Global Omega(t), Delta(t) pulses + local detuning SLMs
- **Coherence**: T2* ~ 4 us
- **Readout**: Fluorescence imaging, single-site resolved
- **Fidelity**: State prep ~99.5%, readout ~99.5%
- **Cost**: $0.01/shot via AWS Braket

**Key platform distinction**: Aquila does NOT have single-qubit gates. MERA must be encoded as geometric arrangement of tweezers exploiting blockade constraint as natural isometry.

## Analog MERA Protocol

### Atom Layout (16 boundary qubits, 3 layers)
```
Layer 3 (UV):  o--o--o--o--o--o--o--o  [8 boundary qubits, 5um spacing]
                 \/ \/ \/ \/           [blockade pairs, Rb=9.76um]
Layer 2:          o--o--o--o           [4 bond qubits, offset 10um]
                    \/ \/
Layer 1 (IR):        o--o             [2 coarse qubits]
                       \/
                        o             [1 bulk qubit]
Total atoms: 8+4+2+1+9 ancilla = 24 atoms
```

### Pulse Sequence (estimated 2.8 us total)
1. **State prep** (~0.3 us): Initialize all atoms to |g> via optical pumping
2. **Layer 3 isometry** (~0.8 us): Global pi/2 pulse + local detuning to entangle boundary pairs via blockade
3. **Layer 2 isometry** (~0.8 us): Drive bond qubits into entangled state with layer-3 pairs
4. **Layer 1 isometry** (~0.7 us): Final coarse-graining pulse
5. **Hold** (~0.2 us): Allow coherent evolution to complete
6. **Readout**: Fluorescence image all sites

**T2* headroom**: 4.0 - 2.8 = 1.2 us (tight but viable; Floquet echo recommended to extend effective T2)

### Analog Error Budget
- Rabi frequency inhomogeneity: ~1% (SLM uniformity)
- Blockade leakage: ~0.5% per pair
- Accumulated 3-layer: ~3-5% coherent error
- Decoherence contribution: ~1-2%
- **Total estimated error**: 4-8% (compared to 0.57% on Helios)

## Force Measurement Protocol

1. **Prepare ground state**: Run variational analog sweep over {Omega_max, Delta_max, pulse_duration} to minimize energy expectation. Use 500 shots per parameter point.
2. **Inject hologrons**: Apply local detuning pulse at boundary sites j and j+dr to create boundary-primary excitations (sigma_j = Ry excitation on site j)
3. **Measure force**: F(dr) = E_dual(j, j+dr) - E_0 - 2*(E_single - E_0)
4. **Shot budget**: 1000 shots per dr value, dr = 1..8 -> 8,000 shots total
5. **Expected signal**: F(dr) ~ A * dr^{-4}, with A ~ -8.3 (from 16q exact, SIM-2026-015d)

## Classical SDR Baseline

**At the same frequency band as Rydberg control lasers (780nm probe, 480nm coupling):**
- These are optical frequencies, not RF -- SDR is not applicable
- Classical baseline for this experiment: **Fabry-Perot cavity spectroscopy** (classical EIT without Rydberg state)
  - Equipment: two ECDLs (780nm + 480nm), Rb vapor cell, photodetector
  - Cost: ~$15-20k (commercial laser system)
  - Sensitivity: classical EIT linewidth ~MHz; Rydberg AT splitting resolves sub-kHz signals
  - **Rydberg advantage**: ~1000x frequency resolution from AT splitting vs classical EIT

**No classical analog for quantum MERA force measurement** -- the force F(dr) requires quantum entanglement across the chain. The classical "baseline" is the exact diagonalization result (SIM-2026-015d) used as ground truth.
