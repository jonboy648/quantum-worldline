## 0. Executive summary

- The PRD manuscript and the local SciPost v5 are **scientifically identical**. The entire diff is
  document-class plumbing plus one dropped Zenodo DOI line and four blank lines. No number,
  table, sentence of physics, or caveat differs. (§1 below.)
- **63 substantive claims** were extracted and traced. **41 verify cleanly** against a named record.
  **10 have a number that disagrees with the record.** **7 have no record at all.** **5 are
  novelty/priority claims with zero recorded prior-art evidence** (direct §8 violation).
- The single most consequential defect is **not** a physics error: it is a **bibliography whose
  author given-names and titles are wrong in at least four entries** relative to the project's own
  catalogued paper records (`Anand2023`, `BohraSivaramakrishnan2025`, `Roux2026`,
  `SahayLukinCotler2025`), and an **external-validation claim (`WangHe2026` "analytically proved
  t_trans = π") that the project's own record of that arXiv ID does not contain**, repeated three
  times including in the Conclusion.
- The second most consequential is a **hardware section built on two mutually inconsistent internal
  records** (532 vs 286 parameters; depth 30 vs 15/25; IBM 99.5% vs 99.8%; 57 gates vs 140 gates)
  while the project's own feasibility simulation (`SIM-2026-019`, verdict `PARTIALLY_REFUTED`)
  never surfaces.
- The **uncertainty on every headline number is a bare covariance-matrix 1σ with no stated method**,
  and in the α = 3.92 case the project's own bootstrap record gives σ = 0.63 — **4× larger** than the
  ±0.16 the abstract quotes. The abstract's "inconsistent with scalar exchange (α = 2)" reads as a
  12σ exclusion at ±0.16 and only ~2.2σ at the bootstrap σ.
